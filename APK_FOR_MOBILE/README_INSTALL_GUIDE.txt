HOW TO SEND & INSTALL ON YOUR MOBILE PHONE:
-----------------------------------------------------

OPTION 1: WhatsApp Web (Easiest - 10 Seconds)
1. Open WhatsApp Web on your PC.
2. Drag and drop 'RevMoto-Admin-v1.0.apk' into your chat (or your own number).
3. On your Android phone, tap the file in WhatsApp.
4. Tap 'Install' (If prompted, tap 'Settings' -> 'Allow from this source').
5. Open 'Rev Moto Admin' and allow notification permission for real-time order alerts!

OPTION 2: USB Cable or Google Drive
1. Copy 'RevMoto-Admin-v1.0.apk' to your phone via USB cable or upload to Google Drive.
2. Tap the file in your phone's 'Files' or 'Downloads' app.
3. Tap 'Install'.

OPTION 3: Direct Download from Website
1. Open https://salikleo.github.io/Rev-Moto-Bike-Gear-Website/admin/ on your mobile browser.
2. Tap the blue 'Download App' button at the top right.
3. Tap 'Download APK File' (or Mirror Link).
4. Tap 'Install' once downloaded.
