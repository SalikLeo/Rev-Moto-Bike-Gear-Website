About Rev Moto

Rev Moto exists to solve a simple challenge: How to engineer protective motorcycle armor that can withstand asphalt slides at high speeds while feeling lightweight, breathable, and razor-sharp in appearance.

Headquartered in Sialkot, Pakistan — the global manufacturing capital for precision racing leathers and sports equipment — we fuse authentic leathercraft with aerospace composite materials: molded carbon fiber, non-Newtonian D3O armor, and ballistic Cordura matrices.

Our Standards

OUR 4 CORE STANDARDS

Standards every Rev Moto product must satisfy before leaving our technical workshop.

Maximum Impact Absorption

Impact force is dispersed across composite armor plates and memory polymers rather than transferred to your body.

All-Weather Airflow & Comfort

Riders need to stay cool and alert. Continuous airflow channeling is engineered into every seam for warm-weather riding.

Ergonomics & Touch Controls

Pre-curved sleeves, flexible armor pockets, and conductive fingertips make daily riding and navigation effortless.

Direct Workshop Value
