PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2026 Collection Launch

1. Information We Collect

When you visit Rev Moto or place an order for motorcycle gear, we collect information necessary to process and deliver your shipment. This includes:

  • Customer contact details (Full name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address).
  • Delivery location details (Street address, city, postal code, special courier notes).
  • Order specifics (Items purchased, sizes, transaction IDs, payment preferences).
  • Anonymous technical browsing data (Browser type, device resolution, pages visited).

2. How Your Information Is Used

We use your information exclusively for legitimate e-commerce operations, specifically:

  • Printing dispatch air waybills and fulfilling deliveries to your address.
  • Sending order verification notices and live WhatsApp tracking updates.
  • Assisting with rider sizing advice, warranty queries, or exchange requests.
  • Protecting our store from fraud and verifying Cash on Delivery orders.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

Rev Moto does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share delivery details strictly with licensed courier logistics providers (such as TCS, Leopard Courier, Call Courier) solely for package transit. All electronic data transmissions are encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption.

4. Your Rights & Data Contact

You have the full right to request copies of your order history, update your contact preferences, or request data deletion from our marketing newsletters. To exercise these rights, please reach out to our privacy officer at revmoto.pk@gmail.com.
